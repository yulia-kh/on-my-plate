# 🍽️ On My Plate

> Snap a picture, log your food. Fast, simple, and free.

---

## 💡 Why We Are Building This

Managing Type 1 Diabetes is hard work every single day. This project started because a close friend told me how hard it is to get her teenage son to track his food.

Most food apps require too much typing, searching, and clicking. Teenagers get tired of doing this for every meal and stop logging their food. When they skip logging, it becomes very hard for parents to help them calculate the right insulin doses and keep their blood sugar at a safe level.

There are many nutrition apps available today, but they have two big problems:

1. **They are too slow:** They require too many steps and clicks to add a single meal.
2. **They cost too much:** The apps that can scan food with a camera usually require an expensive monthly subscription.

**On My Plate** fixes this. Our goal is to make food tracking as fast and simple as possible.

### How it works:

- **One Click:** Open the app, snap a photo, and the AI automatically names the food.
- **Fast UI:** The app is lightweight and saves the meal instantly to your history.
- **100% Free:** No paywalls and no subscription fees.

We are starting with a fast food logger, but we are building it so it can grow into a full tool for tracking nutrition and blood sugar in the future.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

We are keeping the code simple and lightweight:

- **Frontend:** HTML, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript (using the browser's live camera)
- **AI Brain:** Image Recognition API (like OpenAI Vision or Google Vision)
- **Hosting:** Vercel or Netlify
